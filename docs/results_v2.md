## ⚠️ Data status — read first

Energy prices (price scenario A) and the demand charge (Rs 350/kVA/month) are **PENDING stand-ins** (tariff v2 status: PENDING); daily figures carry 1/30 of the monthly demand charge on the day's peak kVA (ASSUMPTION). Rs figures are therefore not headline results; comparisons between methods on identical inputs are meaningful. Campus loads are area-based estimates.

## Setup

Every method runs on the same days, data and constraints and is settled by the same `model.settle()`. v2 re-plans 24 h ahead every 15-min slot (dispatch: LP + consensus ADMM every slot; commitment master re-optimised at the start of every clock hour, carried forward in between). Benders stops at a 0.5 % bound gap or 6 iterations. Rows marked *solar forecast* use the clear-sky × clearness forecast instead of actual solar; all other v2 rows see actual solar over the horizon, like the MILP baselines. The optimum is the central MILP with the demand charge (B4-DC, 0.1 % MIP gap, 15-min binaries, continuous flows); v2's hourly commitments are a restriction of it.

## Decentralisation: old binary ADMM vs ADMM on the convex LP

| Method | ADMM runs converged | mean iterations |
|---|---:|---:|
| Old Q-Grid (binary ADMM + QAOA) | 2 % | 15 |
| v2 hybrid, exact master | 95 % | 53 |
| v2 hybrid, QAOA master | 85 % | 134 |

v1's ADMM agrees on discrete tie-line levels (non-convex): 'converged' = all neighbours agreed within the iteration cap in a slot. v2's ADMM runs on the continuous LP subproblem (convex): 'converged' = every cable's copies within 1 kW and the consensus moving < 1 kW per iteration. Each building then fixes the agreed flows and re-solves its own LP so the applied plan balances exactly.
