## Headline (generated from the results)

**Q-Grid lands 2.3% from the best possible plan; the closest alternative without a central controller, Particle swarm optimisation, lands 23.5% (paired Wilcoxon p < 0.001).** Among methods that work without a central controller, Q-Grid is closest to the best possible plan on 3 of 5 campuses; elsewhere (BDG2 university site 'Bull' (Texas); BDG2 university site 'Cockatoo' (New York)) another method is closer.

## Protocol

- **Datasets** (same for every method): RVCE, Bengaluru (case study); BDG2 university site 'Fox' (Arizona); BDG2 university site 'Bull' (Texas); BDG2 university site 'Cockatoo' (New York); CU-BEMS office building, Bangkok (7 floors). Licences and mappings: `data/SOURCES.md`; asset sizing rule for sites without asset data: `qgrid/datasets.py`.
- **Days**: 30–30 evaluation days per dataset (seeded sample across the year); a seeded 20 % of days carry one 1–3 h unplanned grid outage. 365 days for RVCE is pending RVCE meter data.
- **Same information**: every non-oracle method uses the same gradient-boosted solar and load forecasts (day-ahead methods: the 00:00 forecast; MPC methods: rolling). Status quo and the rule-based controller react to the current measurement only. The best possible plan (central MILP with perfect information) is the only oracle and is shown as a reference line.
- **Same objective and VoLL**: energy + diesel fuel + value of lost load (non-critical Rs 40/kWh, critical Rs 1000/kWh — ASSUMPTION, see docs/consistency.md) + terminal value. No method optimises the demand charge; it is reported in the bill.
- **Communication failure**: one seeded 3-hour campus network outage per day (06:00–21:00). Central MPC cannot reach its coordinator and devices fall back to the rule-based controller; Q-Grid's buildings lose their neighbours and keep optimising alone (each runs its own solver).
- **Statistics**: mean ± standard deviation over days; 95 % t-intervals in `summary.csv`; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against Q-Grid on the same days in `wilcoxon.csv`; certified gap from proven lower bounds (docs/math.md).
- Same machine, one process pool, fixed seeds. Reinforcement learning (PPO) was optional and is **not included**.
