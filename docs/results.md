## Honest interpretation

- **Against the optimum.** On the 30-day set (scenario A) OURS is 7.63 % above B4 and B5
  8.26 %; B3 (no sharing) is 8.20 % and the status quo
  14.41 %. The decentralised method approaches but does not reach the centralised optimum.
- **Why the gap.** Discrete tie-line levels (B4 is continuous), 45-minute local horizons with a local cost-to-go instead of a
  joint day-ahead plan, and ADMM with binaries: only 48.7 % of slots reached full agreement within 15 iterations
  (mean 8.7 iterations; 21 oscillation cases detected). Unresolved lines are settled bilaterally or set to
  0 — always feasible, but sharing is lost.
- **QAOA's real contribution.** Local solves on the 6 scenarios: 85,845; answered by QAOA and verified optimal
  66.0 %, by QIEA 25.6 %, by annealing 0.4 %, by exact 8.0 %.
  Mean P(optimum) 0.0897; best-of-256 found the optimum in 75.9 % of solves. Because every
  answer is verified against brute force, OURS and B5 differ only by ties between equally optimal bitstrings.
- **Where Q-Grid helps.** Outage handling: sharing DG and battery power over tie-lines plus the critical-load safety rule
  (see headline). On ordinary days savings are small because the assumed batteries are small relative to MW-scale loads.
- **Battery payback (ASSUMED capex 36,500,000 Rs):** 22.4 years in scenario A
  (saving 4,460 Rs/day vs. the same ADMM without batteries).
- **Search space.** A centralised full-day formulation has N = 4,416 binary variables (10^1,329.3 states);
  each local QUBO has at most 14 qubits.
- **Robustness.** Networked mode (one process per zone, websockets, no coordinator): recovery after a zone crash
  3.16 s (3 s silence detection + re-convergence); all kill runs feasible: yes.

## QAOA scaling (`results/qaoa_scaling.csv`, `results/plots/qaoa_scaling.png`)

At 13 qubits, P(optimum) with transferred parameters is 0.0077 (p=1),
0.0115 (p=2), 0.0149 (p=3) vs. uniform 0.00012. Exhaustive search at
these sizes takes milliseconds: no quantum advantage, as labelled.

## Limitations

PENDING prices, CO2 factor, calendar and campus loads (see top); perfect day-ahead forecasts for every method; QAOA simulated
classically (ideal, noiseless); lossless tie-lines with a fixed rating, no AC power flow; ADMM convergence with binaries is not
guaranteed (handled by bilateral resolution and safe fallbacks, reported above).
